What is processed

DataWhyKept
Your name, email address and group memberships, received from Helmholtz AAI when you sign in To identify your account and send you job notifications Until your account is deleted
Your Blablador API key To submit your jobs and retrieve your results under your own account. Encrypted before storage, never displayed back to you, and never written to logs Until you remove it on the API key page
Your job inputs — sequences, uploaded structures and parameters They are the input to the computation Stored with the job record until the job is deleted
Your results So they remain available after the computing service deletes its own copy On this portal's storage until deleted by an administrator
Job metadata — timestamps, status, GPU time used, and an audit trail of changes to each job To show you your jobs, and to let administrators diagnose failures With the job record

Where your data goes

Job inputs are sent to the molecular computing service at {{ api_base }}, operated by the Jülich Supercomputing Centre, and are processed on its shared storage and GPU nodes. That service keeps inputs and results on shared server storage during processing and for 48 hours afterwards: it is not a service where nothing is written to disk. Do not submit material that may not be stored in this way.

Nothing is sent to any other recipient. The portal sets no advertising or analytics cookies; the only cookies are those needed to keep you signed in and to protect forms against cross-site request forgery.

Your rights

You can remove your API key at any time on the API key page. To obtain a copy of your data, correct it, or have it deleted, contact {{ admin_contact }}. You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority.

Logging

The portal records errors and job state changes. These records do not contain your API key, your sequences or your uploaded structures.
