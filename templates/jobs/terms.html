Who may use this service

BioPortal is provided for scientific work by members of {{ organisation }} and their collaborators. You may use it only with an account issued to you personally. Do not share your account, and do not share the API key you store here.

What you may run

  • Submit only work related to your research.
  • Submit only data you are permitted to process on shared institute infrastructure. Inputs and results are written to shared storage, so do not submit personal data, patient data, or material under a confidentiality obligation that forbids this.
  • Do not attempt to circumvent the per-user job limits, or to use the service to obtain computing capacity for unrelated purposes.
  • Compute is metered against your own API key. You are responsible for the jobs submitted under it.

Security

  • Do not attempt to access other users' jobs, results or credentials.
  • Do not probe, scan or attempt to disrupt the service or the systems behind it.
  • Report anything that looks like a security problem to {{ security_contact }} rather than exploring it.

Suspension

Your access may be suspended or terminated without notice if it is being misused, or during the investigation of a security incident — including where the incident originates elsewhere and your account is implicated. This follows the incident response practices of the operating organisation.

Logging

Sign-ins, sign-outs and failed sign-in attempts are recorded with a timestamp and the originating network address, and job activity is recorded against your account, so that security incidents can be investigated. These records never contain your API key or your submitted sequences and structures. How your data is handled is described in the data privacy statement.

No warranty

This is a research service provided as-is. Results are generated by predictive models and may be wrong, including when the service reports success. Check results before relying on them. Availability is not guaranteed, and results are retained only as described in the privacy statement.
